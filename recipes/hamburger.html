Description

A classic hamburger is a popular fast food item consisting of a grilled or fried beef patty served between a sliced bread roll.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 bun
  • Letuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Pickle
  • Mayo
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard

Steps to make a Hamburger

  1. Preheat the grill or a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into a ball and flatten it into a patty.
  3. Season the patty with salt and pepper.
  4. Place the patty on the grill or skillet and cook for about 4-5 minutes on each side, or until done to your liking.
  5. While the patty is cooking, toast the bun halves if desired.
  6. Assemble the hamburger by placing the cooked patty on the bottom half of the bun.
  7. Add your desired toppings such as lettuce, tomato, onion, pickle, mayo, ketchup, and mustard.
  8. Place the top half of the bun on top and serve immediately.
