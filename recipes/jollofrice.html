Jollof Rice

Home

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, vegetables, and a flavorful tomato-based sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 2 cups vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps to make Jollof rice

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion, garlic, and ginger. Sauté until fragrant.
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and cook until they break down into a sauce.
  5. Stir in the curry powder, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  6. Add the rinsed rice to the pot and stir to coat it with the sauce.
  7. Add 2 cups of water and bring to a boil.
  8. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it simmer for about 20 minutes or until the rice is cooked and fluffy.
  9. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot.
